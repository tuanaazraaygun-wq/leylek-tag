## Ecosystem principle

One approved logo genome propagates to every surface through **tier ladders** — not one PNG stretched everywhere.

```
LOGO GENOME (F2 master + F3 micro)
        │
        ├── App Icon (1024 → masked)
        ├── Splash (centered · arc mass visible)
        ├── Login (hero · 40% viewport)
        ├── Leylek Zeka (eye derivative)
        ├── Watermark (mono · 6% opacity)
        ├── Website (header + OG)
        ├── Markers (arc chassis family)
        ├── QR (high contrast center mark)
        ├── Notification (silhouette only)
        └── Launcher (adaptive safe zone)
```

---

## 1. App Icon

**Mockup spec:**
- Artboard 1024×1024; logo occupies **72%** of safe circle
- Use **F2** optical center — verify iOS squircle + Android adaptive mask
- Background: solid `#0D1117` or subtle radial `#121820` → `#0D1117`
- No text · no gradient on bird @ store icon
- Foreground layer: white stork + cyan arc + eye dot

**Failure to avoid:** B5.2 export — bird too small, arc hairline, reads generic @ home screen

---

## 2. Splash

**Mockup spec:**
- Logo @ **38%** screen width · vertical center −4%
- Presence pulse: arc opacity 0.92 → 1.0 over 600 ms (spec only)
- Metallic hero optional on splash raster tier — vector arc mass mandatory
- Boot sequence: logo → fade → app chrome (no long logo hold >1.2 s)

---

## 3. Login

**Mockup spec:**
- Mark above form · max width 120 pt @ phone
- **F2** master · eye dot pulses once on successful auth (motion spec)
- Pair with Trust White typography — logo never competes with OTP fields
- White theme variant: invert to slate body on `#F5F7FA` panel — arc stays cyan

---

## 4. Leylek Zeka

**Mockup spec:**
- **Eye derivative:** cyan dot + partial arc — not full bird @ chat header
- Empty state: full **F2** logo @ 64 pt + “Leylek Zeka hazır” copy
- Widget trigger: eye-only @ 28 pt — links to logo genome without clutter
- Chat watermark: arc fragment @ 4% opacity behind messages

---

## 5. Watermark

**Mockup spec:**
- Mono white @ **6%** opacity (component applies — not baked in PNG)
- Full silhouette · no eye glow · no arc gradient
- Tile repeat forbidden — single center or corner anchor only
- Muhabbet / trip share sheets: bottom-right · 48 pt max

---

## 6. Website

**Mockup spec:**
- Header: horizontal lockup — symbol only @ 32 px nav · wordmark separate
- Hero: **F2** @ 180 px with orbital arc breathing room
- OG image 1200×630: logo left third · map chrome mock right · `#0D1117` field
- Favicon: **F3** @ 32/16 — eye + arc fragment must survive

## 7. Marker (map)

**Mockup spec:**

## 8. QR Payment / Trust

**Mockup spec:**

## 9. Notification
